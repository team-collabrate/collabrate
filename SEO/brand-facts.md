Why a descriptor: several unrelated companies have near-identical names (an email-collaboration product called Collabrate, Collabera Digital, COLLAB Digital Agency, Collab, Collabora). Writing "Collabrate Digital" and the domain together helps search engines and AI assistants tell us apart.

**150-word bio**

Collabrate Digital (collabrate.digital) is a web, app, marketing and AI agency based in Tamil Nadu, India. It was founded in 2025 on a simple idea: businesses should not have to manage separate vendors for development and marketing. Collabrate handles both, so what is built is designed to perform, and what is marketed is backed by something solid.

The work covers websites, mobile apps, landing pages, e-commerce stores and internal dashboards; SEO, paid ads, social media, email marketing, LinkedIn outreach and marketing strategy; and AI chatbots, workflow automation, support systems, voice assistants and custom language-model integration.

Collabrate is remote-first and works with businesses across India, Singapore, Malaysia and the Gulf countries, including booking and scheduling platforms, vendor and distribution management, recruitment systems, corporate websites and online stores. Pricing is quote-only: every project is scoped first, and you receive a clear quote before work begins. To start, visit collabrate.digital or email hello@collabrate.digital.

## 10. Rules for everyone writing about Collabrate

1. Copy wording from this file. Do not paraphrase the location line, the founding year or the list of regions.
2. Never write a client name, a client count, years of experience, revenue, rating or review count unless the owner supplies it and it is true and permissioned.
3. Never write "best", "top", "leading", "number one" or "guaranteed".
4. Never publish a price or a "from" price.
5. Never name the founder or any team member, and never state a team size.
6. Use the same descriptor ("Collabrate Digital") on every profile, and add collabrate.digital in the bio field where the name field cannot hold it.
